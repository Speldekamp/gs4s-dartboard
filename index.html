Overview

This is the interactive policy dartboard tool developed by Daniël Speldekamp and Ayse Saka-Helmhout for the Horizon Europe GS4S project - Global Strategy for Skills, Migration and Development. It is deliverable 4.3 in task 4.3, and titled “On Target: Sector Pathways to Close Skill Shortages”. More information on the project is available from the project website (gs4s.eu) and CORDIS (https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/101132377).

This figure works with the data also presented in policy brief no. 4, “Closing Europe’s Skills Gap: Sector-Specific Lessons for EU Skills Policy”. The authors of this brief are Ayse Saka-Helmhout, Daniël Speldekamp, Albert Rutten, Mariana Alvarado, Sandra Lavenex, and Arjan Heyma. The full policy brief is available on Zenodo via https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20676417.

The figure below showcases consistent ways in which multinational enterprises in Germany, Estonia, Switzerland and the Netherlands successfully address skill shortages.
