October 2026 · Daniël Speldekamp, Ayse Saka-Helmhout

01Overview

What works when companies can’t find the skilled people they need? This tool shows the combinations of strategies that multinational companies in Germany, Estonia, Switzerland and the Netherlands have used to close skill shortages in four sectors: IT, manufacturing, health and construction.

Choose a sector, then click a wedge to see one pathway: the conditions the companies faced, the strategies they combined, and the lesson for policy. Larger wedges mean more companies took that route.

03About

The Dartboard Tool is based on the forthcoming publication “On Target: Sector Pathways to Close Skill Shortages”, and was developed by Daniël Speldekamp and Ayse Saka-Helmhout. The underlying data is from the policy brief “Closing Europe’s Skills Gap: Sector-Specific Lessons for EU Skills Policy” (Saka-Helmhout et al., 2026). It is part of GS4S – Global Strategy for Skills, Migration and Development, a Horizon Europe project (gs4s.eu, CORDIS).
